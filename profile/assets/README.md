# General Robotics brand artwork

Selected artwork for the public General Robotics organization profile.

- `general-robotics-lockup.png`: company wordmark, red-and-silver Taking Flight emblem, and Engineering Excellence motto.
- `a-life-of-your-own.png`: domestic concept artwork.
- `grand-exposition.png`: exposition concept artwork.

The scenes illustrate the future General Robotics aspires to build. They do not describe a currently available product or its specifications.
