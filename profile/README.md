<h2 align="center">A life of your own.</h2>

General Robotics is pursuing embodied AI: intelligent machines that can understand the physical world and perform useful work within it. Our ambition spans many applications, with a special place for the home.

We believe progress should give people more freedom to pursue what matters to them—to learn, create, care for one another, explore, and build lives of their own choosing.

### Engineering Excellence

Our motto expresses the standard we are working toward: capable machines, thoughtful design, and craftsmanship worthy of the people who will live and work with them.

Inspired by Pittsburgh's tradition of making, the artistry of the great international expositions, and the optimism of early technology, we see beauty and engineering as parts of the same pursuit. We want to build a future that cherishes what people already love while opening new possibilities.
